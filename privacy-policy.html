Section 09

Your NDPR Rights

Under Nigerian data protection law, you possess enforceable rights over your personal data:

Right to Access

Request verification and copies of any personal data we hold.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your data when no longer legally required.

Right to Portability

Receive your information in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, transmit a written notice to info@orchidbyte-solutions.com.ng with the subject line "Data Rights Request". Responses are provided within 30 days without fee.
